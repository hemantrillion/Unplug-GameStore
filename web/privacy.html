Your data, explained.

This notice describes the current development build. The operator must supply their contact information, applicable terms, retention obligations, and jurisdiction-specific disclosures before a public launch.

Accounts

UNPLUG stores your name, email, role, a salted password hash, verification state, and expiring sessions. Passwords are not stored as plaintext. Verification and recovery links expire after one hour. Local development saves these emails in the server’s private mail directory; configured deployments use SMTP.

Games and community activity

Submissions, release digests, reviews, issue reports, deployment history, and audit records are kept to support publishing and recovery. Only owners and authorized reviewers or administrators can access restricted candidates and reports. Display names appear on the online-game leaderboard.

Offline storage and telemetry

Downloaded games and the last catalog are stored on this device. Startup and runtime-failure events contain a random event identifier and release identifier; no advertising identifier is collected. A bounded offline queue retries when connected. Server telemetry is retained for 30 days. Browser data can be removed through My downloads or browser settings.

Advertising and payments

No advertising, payment, or payout provider is active in this build. It collects no card details and does not create paid entitlements.

Your controls

My account provides account export, password changes, session revocation, and account deletion. Deletion anonymizes the account profile, invalidates sessions, and disables owned games. Release and audit records remain for traceability. Previously downloaded games may remain on players’ offline devices. Administrators must transfer operational responsibility before deletion.

Operator responsibilities

Before collecting real customer data, publish operator contact details and final privacy/terms documents, establish backup retention and erasure procedures, and review age, regional and provider requirements. This build is not configured as a child-directed service.
